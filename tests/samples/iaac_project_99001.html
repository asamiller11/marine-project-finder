​​Example Harbour Shoreline Repair​ – Public comments invited

This is a non-designated project on federal lands.

The proposed project

The Applicant proposes to repair a worn section of shoreline protection at the Example Harbour terminal, on the north bank of the Sample River in Testville, British Columbia at approximately 49.120000, -123.050000. The work area is an approximately 20 to 25 metre-long section of shoreline where the existing rock protection has eroded.

The work includes removing the worn riprap and building a new riprap revetment along the shoreline. The work will be done from land over about 3 weeks.
