Registration Number: 9002

Sample Cove Navigation Improvement

Proponent: Example Ferry Corp.

Summary of Environmental Assessment Process

Project Description:

The proponent proposes navigation improvements in Sample Cove Harbour to support ferry operations, including widening and deepening the approach channel and turning basin.

Dredged rock would be reused to raise the existing Test Island Breakwater and to build an infill area, which may support the development of new fish habitat in the outer harbour. Up to approximately 300,000 cubic metres of material may be removed.

  • September 15, 2026 – The undertaking was registered.
  • October 20, 2026 – Deadline for public comments.
  • October 30, 2026 – The Minister’s decision is due.
