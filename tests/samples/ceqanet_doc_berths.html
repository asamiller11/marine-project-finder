Document Description
The Proposed Project is located at 383 Pier A Place, Wilmington, CA 90744. It would construct a new, Marine Oil Terminal Engineering and Maintenance Standards (MOTEMS)-compliant berthing facility at Berths 150-151. The existing wharf at Berths 148-149 would be used as a Marine Oil Terminal (MOT) while the new facilities at Berths 150-151 are being built. Future uses at Berths 148-149 would be limited to non-MOT uses such as storage of topside equipment and the occasional mooring of harbor craft to reduce congestion in the channel. Landside facilities, including piping, vapor recovery systems, support systems, and tankage, would be modified and upgraded to support the new Berths 150-151 loading platform. In addition, the Proposed Project includes consideration of up to 40 years of continued operation as a MOT at Berths 148-151, currently proposed via a new 20-year entitlement with two optional 10-year extensions.
