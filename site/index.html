How to read this list

Every notice is scored automatically from the words and sizes in it, then placed in a tier:

  • Strong match: Score 60 or more. Several signals of habitat work, such as offsetting, new habitat, or large in-water works.
  • Possible: Score 35 to 59. Some signals. Worth a look.
  • Weak: Score 20 to 34. Few signals. Often routine maintenance. Hidden unless you tick "Show weaker matches".

Notices scoring under 20, quarries and pits, and withdrawn projects are not listed.

A flag means a notice may involve marine habitat work. It is not a statement that mitigation or compensation is required. Items marked "Reviewed by a person" have been checked; the rest are automatic. Always read the official notice.
