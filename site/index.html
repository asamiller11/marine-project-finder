How it works

Updated every week. Last update November 22, 2026.

  1. We scan public notices

    Official permit and environmental assessment notices from 3 countries, read every week.

  2. We score each project

    Clear rules on the type of work, habitat words in the notice and its size give each project a fit.

  3. You get a weekly list

    Strong and possible fits, with who to contact, how to get in and the deadline to beat.
